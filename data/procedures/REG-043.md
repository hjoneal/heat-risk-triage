# Environmental compliance for oil-filled plant

Oil-filled plant is subject to containment, reporting and waste handling obligations.

## Containment

Every oil-filled asset above the threshold volume is provided with secondary containment sized to hold the full oil charge plus an allowance for firewater and rainfall. Bund integrity is inspected annually and after any works within the bund.

Bund drain valves are held closed and locked. An open bund drain valve found at inspection is recorded as an immediate defect and closed at the time of discovery.

Standing water in a bund reduces the available containment volume and is to be removed through the interceptor, with the discharge visually checked for oil.

## Reporting

A release that reaches a watercourse, a surface water drain or unmade ground outside the containment is reportable to the environmental regulator within the statutory notification period. Internal notification to the environmental duty officer is immediate.

A release retained within the bund is recorded internally and is not externally reportable, but the underlying defect enters the defect register under SOP-016.

## Waste

Waste oil, contaminated absorbent and contaminated soil are hazardous waste and are consigned accordingly. Consignment notes are retained for three years.

## Inspection findings

Evidence of oil staining on a plinth or bund floor is recorded as an environmental observation as well as an asset condition finding. Both records are required: one drives the repair, the other supports the compliance position.

Repeated small releases from the same asset are aggregated for reporting purposes and may cross a reporting threshold that no single release would reach.
