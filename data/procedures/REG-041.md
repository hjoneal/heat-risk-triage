# Asset condition reporting obligations

The licence requires the periodic reporting of asset condition and of the defect backlog against the network's principal plant.

## Scope

Reporting covers all substation transformers rated 10 MVA and above. For each asset the return records age in service, the date and outcome of the most recent inspection, the current condition assessment, and the count and age of open defects.

## Condition assessment

The condition assessment is derived from the recorded inspection findings, the oil condition results and the loading history. It is not a subjective judgement and is not to be adjusted to improve a reported position.

Where an assessment has changed materially since the previous return, the return records the reason for the change.

## Defect backlog

Open defects are reported by category and by age. Defects open beyond their target completion date are reported separately, with the reason for the delay and the revised target date.

A defect that has been carried forward across more than two review cycles without work is reported as a deferred defect and requires a named accountable engineer.

## Data quality

The return must reconcile to the asset register and to the work management system. Discrepancies are corrected in the source system, not in the return.

Inspection findings that were not recorded at the time of inspection cannot be added retrospectively to a return. Where an omission is identified, it is recorded as a data quality exception.

## Retention

Inspection records, oil results and defect histories are retained for the life of the asset plus six years. Records supporting a condition assessment used in a regulatory return are retained for the period covered by the return and the following six years.
