# Outstanding work order review and defect close-out

Deferred work accumulates. This procedure governs the periodic review of open work orders against an asset and the close-out of completed remedial activity.

## Review cycle

The open defect register for each asset is reviewed quarterly and before any forecast heat event affecting the district. The review establishes, for every open item: what was raised, when, why it was deferred, and whether the reason for deferral still holds.

An item deferred for parts availability that has been open for more than two review cycles is escalated. An item carried forward with no recorded reason is escalated immediately.

## Priority ahead of a heat event

Where a heat event is forecast, outstanding work is re-prioritised against its effect on the unit's ability to shed heat. In descending order of priority:

1. Failed or degraded forced cooling
2. Obstructed ventilation or airflow
3. Oil level below minimum, or an active leak
4. Oil condition defects awaiting reconditioning
5. All other outstanding items

Work in categories 1 to 3 is to be completed before the event begins wherever crew capacity allows. Category 4 items that cannot be completed require a loading restriction instead.

## Close-out

A defect is closed only when the remedial work has been completed and verified on site. Verification is by the person completing the work or by the next inspection, and is recorded with the date and the work order reference.

Do not close a defect on the basis that it was not observed at a later visit. An unobserved defect is recorded as not observed; a closed defect asserts that work was done.

## Reporting

Open items outstanding beyond their target date are reported under REG-041. The count of open items against an asset is a condition indicator in its own right and is carried into pre-event triage.
