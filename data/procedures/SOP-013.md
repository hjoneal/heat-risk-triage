# Oil sampling and dissolved gas analysis

Routine oil sampling establishes the dielectric condition of the insulating oil and, through dissolved gas analysis, gives early warning of developing thermal and electrical faults inside the tank.

## Sampling interval

Units rated 50 MVA and above are sampled annually. Units below 50 MVA are sampled on a two-year cycle unless a prior result was marginal, in which case the unit returns to an annual cycle until two consecutive results fall within specification.

## Sampling method

Draw the sample from the bottom drain valve after flushing sufficient oil to clear the standing volume in the valve body. Use clean, dry sampling apparatus. Record the top oil temperature and the ambient temperature at the time of sampling; both are required for interpretation.

Samples taken during or immediately after a heat event are not comparable with the routine series and must be annotated accordingly.

## Interpretation

Dielectric strength below the specified minimum indicates moisture ingress or particulate contamination. A marginal result is not a pass; schedule a retest and treat the unit as condition-limited until the retest returns within specification.

Dissolved gas results are interpreted against the established trend for the individual unit, not against a fleet threshold. A rising trend in ethylene or acetylene indicates a thermal fault involving oil, and warrants investigation regardless of the absolute concentration. Hydrogen alone is commonly associated with partial discharge.

## Action on a failed sample

A failed dielectric result requires the oil to be reconditioned or replaced before the unit is exposed to sustained high loading. Where a heat event is forecast within the remedial window, apply the loading restriction in MG-021 until the work is complete and the retest has returned within specification.

Record the sample reference, the result and the resulting action against the asset. An open sampling defect is an outstanding remedial item for the purposes of pre-event triage.
