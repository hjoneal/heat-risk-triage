# Winter storm preparation and site access

This procedure covers the preparation of substation sites for the winter period and the arrangements required to keep them reachable during a storm.

## Access routes

Confirm before the winter period that the access route to each site is passable by the heaviest vehicle likely to be required, and that gate locks and hinges have been treated against freezing. A padlock that has frozen has delayed restoration on sites where every other arrangement was in place.

Identify sites where the access route is impassable under snow and record the alternative arrangement, including any landowner permission required for an alternative approach.

## Gritting and treatment

Salt bins are to be filled and their contents confirmed dry and free-flowing before the winter period. Treat walkways, steps and the approach to the control building. Do not treat within bunded areas, where the run-off enters the containment.

## Standby arrangements

Confirm the standby roster, the on-call engineer for each district and the escalation route for the storm period. Confirm that satellite or radio communication is available for sites without reliable mobile coverage.

## Site security during a storm

Wind-driven debris entering a compound has caused faults on plant that was otherwise unaffected by the storm. Before the winter period, walk the boundary of each site and remove or secure anything within the fence line capable of being lifted by high wind.

Confirm fence integrity and that gates latch positively.

## Battery systems

Battery performance falls with falling cell temperature. Confirm the battery room heating and prove the charger and the alarm output at the autumn visit. A depleted battery on a site that has lost supply removes the ability to operate switchgear at the point it is most needed.
