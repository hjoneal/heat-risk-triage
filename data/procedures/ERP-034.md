# Oil spill containment and response

This procedure covers the response to a release of insulating oil from substation plant.

## Immediate containment

Establish the source and stop the release where it is safe to do so. Do not enter the safety clearance of energised plant to reach a leak; arrange isolation first.

Confirm that the bund is retaining the release and that the bund drain valve is closed. Where the bund drain has been left open, close it immediately and check the downstream interceptor.

Deploy absorbent material to any release that has escaped containment. Prevent entry to surface water drains as the first priority.

## Assessment

Estimate the volume released from the level change in the conservator and the extent of the ground contamination. Record the time of discovery, the time of containment and the estimated volume.

Determine whether the release has reached a watercourse, a surface water drain or unmade ground. Any of these is a reportable incident under REG-043 and the environmental duty officer is to be notified within the reporting window.

## Recovery

Recover free oil by pump or absorbent. Contaminated absorbent and soil are disposed of as hazardous waste against a consignment note.

Do not wash the area down. Washing spreads the contamination and moves it into the drainage system.

## Asset condition after a spill

A unit that has lost a material volume of oil has lost both dielectric strength and cooling capacity. Do not return it to normal loading until the level has been restored, the leak corrected and an oil sample returned within specification under SOP-013.

Where the release occurred during a heat event, apply a loading restriction under MG-021 until the sample result is available.
