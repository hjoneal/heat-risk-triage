# Reliability reporting and customer interruption records

Interruptions to customer supply are recorded and reported against the licence reliability standards.

## What is recorded

Every interruption is recorded with its start time, restoration time, the number of customers affected, the cause category and the asset involved. Interruptions shorter than the reporting threshold are recorded but reported separately.

The customer count is taken from the connectivity model at the time of the interruption, not from a nominal figure held against the asset.

## Cause categorisation

Cause is assigned from the investigation, not from the initial field report. An interruption arising from a transformer failure during a period of high ambient temperature is categorised by the underlying failure mechanism, with the weather conditions recorded as a contributing factor.

Categorising a heat-related failure as a weather event where the underlying cause was a known and deferred condition defect is a misreporting and is corrected on identification.

## Severe weather

Interruptions occurring during a declared severe weather event may be reported under the exceptional event provisions. A heat event does not automatically qualify. The qualification test is applied to the event as a whole and is not available for individual asset failures within an otherwise normal period.

## Use in asset prioritisation

The number of customers connected to an asset determines the consequence of its failure and is a legitimate input to the prioritisation of pre-event work. It is a measure of impact, not of likelihood, and the two are recorded and reasoned about separately.

Historical interruption records against an individual asset are retained and inform its condition assessment under REG-041.
