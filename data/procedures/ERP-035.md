# Mobile substation deployment

Mobile plant provides temporary capacity where a substation transformer has failed or must be removed from service during a period of high demand.

## Availability

Two mobile units are held in the region. Availability is confirmed at the start of the heat season and re-confirmed at the 24-hour point of the readiness playbook in ERP-031. Both units cannot be committed to the same district without regional authorisation.

## Site suitability

Confirm before commitment: access route and turning capability for the transport, a level hardstanding within cable reach of the terminations, an available earth connection of adequate rating, and the voltage and vector group match.

Site suitability is established during the heat season readiness review, not at the point of deployment. A site assessed only when the unit has already failed will delay restoration by hours.

## Deployment

Deployment is authorised by the duty operations manager. Expect a minimum of six hours from authorisation to energisation for a prepared site, and considerably longer where cable work or earthing modification is required.

## Rating during a heat event

Mobile units are rated for their own ambient assumptions and are frequently sited without the enclosure ventilation of a permanent installation. Apply the ambient de-rating in MG-021, and take account of the fact that a trailer-mounted unit in direct sun experiences a materially higher effective ambient than the shade temperature.

Confirm that the cooling fans on the mobile unit have been proved before it leaves the depot.

## Return

Return the unit to the depot within 48 hours of the permanent asset being restored, and complete the post-deployment inspection and oil sample before it is declared available again.
