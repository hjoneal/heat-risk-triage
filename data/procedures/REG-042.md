# Maintenance interval compliance

Maintenance intervals for substation transformers are set by policy and are subject to audit.

## Intervals

Routine visual inspection is carried out at six-monthly intervals. Detailed inspection, including cooling system proving and thermographic survey, is annual. Oil sampling follows the interval in SOP-013. Major maintenance requiring an outage follows the interval set for the individual asset class.

An interval is measured from the date of the previous completed activity, not from its scheduled date. Completing a late activity does not reset the schedule to the original date.

## Tolerance

Routine activities may be completed within a tolerance of 10 percent of the interval without being recorded as late. Beyond the tolerance the activity is recorded as overdue and enters the compliance return.

Deferral beyond twice the interval requires documented authorisation from the responsible engineer, with an assessment of the risk accepted and a revised completion date.

## Overdue activity and heat exposure

An asset with overdue detailed inspection has an unverified cooling system. Where such an asset falls within the area of a forecast heat event, the inspection is brought forward in preference to accepting the exposure; see ERP-031.

The time elapsed since the last completed maintenance is carried as a condition indicator in pre-event triage, since it bounds how current the recorded condition of the asset can be.

## Audit

The compliance position is reported quarterly. Evidence for a completed activity is the signed record in the work management system. An activity recorded as complete without a corresponding record is an audit exception and is investigated.
