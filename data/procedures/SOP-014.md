# Pre-event cooling system inspection

This procedure applies to units with forced cooling ahead of a forecast heat event. It is to be completed before the event begins, not during it.

## Fan operation

Run each cooling stage in turn from the control cabinet using the manual test facility. Confirm that every fan in the bank starts, runs up to speed and continues to run for the duration of the test. Record any fan that fails to start, runs slowly, or is noisy in operation.

A seized fan removes a defined fraction of the unit's cooling capacity. Where a fan bank is running with one or more fans failed, the unit is to be treated as de-rated for the duration of the event; apply the guidance in MG-021.

## Control system

Verify that the cooling control thermostat calls each stage at the correct top oil temperature and that the stage sequence is correct. A chattering contactor causes the cooling stage to cycle intermittently and is to be treated as a failed stage rather than a minor defect.

Check that the control supply is healthy and that the cooling system is not left in manual from a previous intervention.

## Radiator condition

Inspect the radiator fins for fouling by dust, pollen, seed or insect debris. Fouling reduces heat transfer across the whole bank and develops gradually enough that it is easily missed between visits. Where fouling is present across more than a third of the bank, arrange cleaning under SOP-018 before the event.

Check that no fin is bent flat against its neighbour and that no radiator is isolated at its valves from previous work.

## Recording

Record the outcome of every stage test, the number of fans proved, and any defect found. Where a defect cannot be cleared before the event begins, note the residual cooling capacity so that the operations desk can apply the correct loading restriction.
